# Product Context & Human Model

## Product Philosophy
- **Quality over Speed:** The MVP must be highly polished. We are not rushing a buggy product to market; what we build must work flawlessly.
- **Non-Negotiable:** Visual Polish. The application must look premium and stunning at first glance.
- **Pragmatic Scoping:** We are willing to heavily cut scope (e.g., offloading chat to Discord) in order to maintain the high quality and visual polish of the core features that remain.

## User Model
- **Target Audience:** Highly stressed students preparing for exams. 
- **Tolerance:** Zero patience. They will not forgive AI hallucinations, clunky UI, or confusing interactions. The experience must be frictionless and trustworthy immediately.

## Proxy Guidelines
- If a technical decision compromises visual polish or introduces risk of AI hallucination, reject it.
- If a feature cannot be delivered with high quality within the timeframe, cut the feature rather than lowering the quality bar.
- Always prioritize a premium, responsive user interface.
